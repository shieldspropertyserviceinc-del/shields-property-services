Snow clearing for landlords and property managers in Transcona — every property on the route, tenants with safe access, and one simple monthly invoice.

WHY LANDLORDS HAND US THE SHOVEL.

  • Safe access, every snowfall. Driveways and walkways cleared within 15 hours of the snowfall ending, all season — tenants come and go safely, and the property stays accessible between tenancies and for showings.
  • Same pricing as homeowners. Season plans from $150/mo per property — Essential (6 cm), Complete (4 cm), Premium (2 cm). No landlord markup, no per-door surprises.
  • One invoice, every property. All your Transcona properties on a single monthly invoice — out on the 1st, due the 15th. Pay by e-transfer, debit, or credit.
  • You never get the 6 AM call. Once a property is on the route, clearing happens automatically when its trigger is met — no tenant has to ask, and neither do you.

WHAT’S INCLUDED.

The same plans our homeowners get, applied per property:

  • Driveway and walkway clearing on your plan’s trigger, November to March
  • Windrow clearing included on Complete and Premium ($25/visit add-on on Essential)
  • Ice scraping included on Complete and Premium
  • Sanding included on Premium; $15 per application on the other plans
  • Free estimate for every property — online from the address, or in-person

GETTING STARTED.

Send us the address — one property or the whole list. We confirm each one is in our Transcona service area, estimate it free, and put it on the route.
