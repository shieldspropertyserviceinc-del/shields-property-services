GIFT A SEASON.

Give someone in Transcona a winter without shovelling. A full season of snow clearing, paid for upfront — driveway and walkway cleared within 15 hours of every snowfall, November to March.

🎁 A full season, paid upfront⏱ Within 15 hours of snowfall📍 Transcona

WHO IT'S FOR.

Adult kids buying for their parents. A neighbour buying for the neighbour who can't shovel anymore. Family buying for someone recovering, working long hours, or living alone. If they live in Transcona and dread the next storm, this is the gift that actually gets used all winter.

HOW IT WORKS.

Step 1

PICK A PLAN

Essential, Complete, or Premium, below.

Step 2

TELL US WHO IT'S FOR

Their address in Transcona, and the best contact for them. We'll do a free estimate, online or in person, just like any other booking.

Step 3

PAY FOR THE SEASON UPFRONT

They're covered for the whole season, November to March. No shovelling, no calling around after each storm, no worrying about the next one.

IF THEY'RE 55 OR OLDER.

The 10% senior discount stacks on the early-bird prices — a full season is Essential $607.50, Complete $787.50, Premium $1,147.50. Just mention it when you sign up. The disability discount is also 10% off, on monthly plans and services — it doesn't stack on early-bird prepay, and it doesn't combine with the senior discount. It's 10% either way, never 20%.

WHAT THEY GET.

Driveway and walkway cleared within 15 hours of snowfall ending, on their plan's trigger. Windrow clearing included on Complete and Premium, $25 a visit as an add-on on Essential. Ice scraping included on Complete and Premium only. Monthly invoices go out on the 1st and are due by the 15th if you choose monthly instead of paying the season upfront — contact us and we'll set it up that way.

WHAT WE NEED FROM YOU.

The recipient's address, their name, and a phone or email where we can reach them. That's it — we'll take it from there, and keep it straightforward for the whole family. Payments by e-transfer, debit, or credit.

GOOD TO KNOW.

Transcona-first service area — Gunn Rd north, Dugald Rd south, Plessis Rd west, Perimeter Hwy east, as guidelines. The season runs November to March. Prefer to pay per visit instead? That's $45 a visit, cleared within 12 hours of the request.
