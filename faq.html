QUESTIONS, ANSWERED.

The stuff Transcona neighbours ask us most.

How do the snowfall triggers work?

We watch the forecast. When snowfall reaches your plan's trigger — 6 cm for Essential, 4 cm for Complete, 2 cm for Premium — we come out and clear within 12 hours of the snow stopping.

What exactly gets cleared each visit?

Your driveway, your walkway, and snow brushed off up to two vehicles parked in the driveway. Windrow clearing (the ridge the city plow leaves) is included with Complete and Premium; Essential can add it for $25 per visit.

What if I'm not home when it snows?

No problem — we clear whether you're home or not. That's the whole point: wake up or come home to a clear driveway.

Will your equipment damage my driveway or lawn?

We work damage-conscious on every visit. Professional, reliable, damage-free isn't a slogan — it's the standard.

How and when do I pay?

E-transfer, debit, or credit. Invoices go out monthly and are due within 15 days.

When does the season run?

November through March, every winter.

Which areas do you serve?

Transcona, Winnipeg — see the service area map on our Contact page. Just outside it? Ask us; we'll give you a straight answer.

How do I get started?

Fill in the booking form on our Book page, or request a free in-person estimate. No pressure, no obligation.
