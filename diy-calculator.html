How the math works

Your DIY cost is simple: minutes per snowfall × snowfalls per season × your hourly rate. Plan costs are full-season totals (monthly rate × 5 months, November–March); per-visit is $45 × your snowfall count. The calculator does not count the things money cannot measure: sore backs, 6 AM alarms in −30°C cold, missed work, or wrestling the plow windrow at the end of the driveway.
